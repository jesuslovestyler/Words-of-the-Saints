St. Joseph

Scripture records not one word from him. His whole saying is his obedience: he simply did what the angel said, every time. Some brothers speak loudest by silence.

Most of these words are quoted from the saints’ own writings, letters, and recorded sayings. A few beloved lines come down to us by tradition rather than a surviving text — those are marked attributed or reported, so you always know which kind you’re holding.

The cloud of witnesses is still growing. So is this page.
